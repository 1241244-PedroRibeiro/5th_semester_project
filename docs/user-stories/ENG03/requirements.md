# ENG03 - As a Project Manager, I want each team member to assess their own contribution and the contributions of the other team members.

## 1. Requirements Engineering

### 1.1. User Story Description
As a Project Manager, I want each team member to assess their own contribution and the contributions of the other team members at the end of the sprint, so that individual participation, collaboration, and contribution to the team's work can be considered during the assessment process.

### 1.2. Customer Specifications and Clarifications

#### From the specification document
> No question-and-answer style clarifications were recorded for this User Story. The requirements below are extracted directly from the acceptance criteria defined in *PART III – Sprint 1 – User Stories*.

### 1.3. Acceptance Criteria

- AC1: Each team member must complete the Sprint 1 self and peer evaluation individually.
- AC2: Each team member must evaluate their own contribution and the contribution of every other member of the team.
- AC3: Evaluations must consider the work and contributions made throughout the sprint, rather than only the final deliverable.
- AC4: Evaluations should be consistent with the available evidence of individual participation and contribution to the project.
- AC5: Each team member is individually responsible for submitting their evaluation through the mechanism defined for the sprint.
- AC6: The evaluation must be submitted within the deadline established for Sprint 1 assessment.

### 1.4. Found out Dependencies
Depends on **ENG02** (Sprint Planning and Project Monitoring), since evaluations must be grounded in the evidence of tasks and contributions tracked during the sprint.

### 1.5 Input and Output Data
Inputs:
- Evidence of individual participation and contribution throughout the sprint
- Team member identities

Outputs:
- Submitted individual self and peer evaluation forms
